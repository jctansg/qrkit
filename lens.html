    Small tool. Simple things.

    Meet your new lens.

    An easy way to get from a QR code to what’s inside.

    1. Point and hold steady

      Enable your camera and fit the code inside the frame. Your result appears as soon as it’s read.

    2. Get a clearer view

      Use + and − to zoom, or flip to another camera. Flash is available on cameras that support it. You can also scan a saved image.

    3. Keep the good stuff

      Scans are added to History. Tap the bookmark on any result to keep it in your collection, even after clearing history.

    Your discoveries are yours.

    Camera frames and images are read on your device and never uploaded. Scans and bookmarks stay in this browser. Clearing your browser’s saved data removes them.

    For camera access, use HTTPS or localhost. Scanning images also works offline when you open this HTML file directly.

    A clean slate?

    This removes your scan history from this browser. Your bookmarks stay saved. This can’t be undone.
